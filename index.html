This Privacy Policy explains how Northstar Studio (“Northstar,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or use our services.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described here. This policy applies to information collected through our website, forms, and other services that link to this policy.

01. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, company, and the contents of messages or requests you send. We may also collect limited technical information, including browser type, device information, approximate location, and pages visited.

Information you provide

Contact details, account information, and communications you send to us.

Information collected automatically

Basic usage, device, and diagnostic information that helps us operate the site.

02. How we use information

We use information to provide and improve our services, respond to requests, send service-related messages, understand how our website is used, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

03. When we share information

We may share information with trusted providers that help us host, secure, analyze, or operate our services. These providers may access information only as needed to perform services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a business transfer.

04. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. You can also unsubscribe from non-essential messages by using the link in the message or contacting us directly. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

05. Cookies and third parties

Our website may use essential cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and understand basic traffic patterns. Third-party services linked from our website have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review them before sharing information.

06. Policy updates

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
